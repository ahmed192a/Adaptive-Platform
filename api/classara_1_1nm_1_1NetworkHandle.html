Overloads with an executor (SWS_ANM_01014, 01015).

ExecutorT is any copy/move-constructible callable that can be invoked with a std::function<void()> and runs it (immediately or later, in any thread): executor(std::function<void()>{...}). The notifier is delivered through it.
