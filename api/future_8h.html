Detailed Description

ara::core::Future<T,E> and ara::core::Promise<T,E> (SWS_CORE_00321 ff.)

Exception-free futures built on ara::core::Result. Differences to the std:: types: the error channel is E (default ErrorCode) and a continuation can be attached with then().

Misuse is reported through FutureErrc (thrown as FutureException, or terminate when ARA_NO_EXCEPTIONS is set): no_state for operations on an empty Future/Promise, future_already_retrieved for a second get_future(), promise_already_satisfied for a second set_value()/SetError(), and a Promise destroyed unfulfilled delivers FutureErrc::broken_promise to its Future (when E is constructible from ErrorCode).
