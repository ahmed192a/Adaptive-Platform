Detailed Description

SWS-shaped facade (ara::crypto::cryp): CryptoProvider and the Ctx classes.

PRAGMATIC SUBSET. The factory names (LoadCryptoProvider, CreateHashFunctionCtx, CreateMessageAuthnCodeCtx, CreateSignerPrivateCtx, CreateVerifierPublicCtx, CreateRandomGeneratorCtx) follow SWS Cryptography; method signatures are simplified (no CryptoPrimitiveId strings, no ReadOnlyMemRegion, no CryptoObject/KeyMaterial hierarchy) and are listed as Partial in doc/spec-conformance/crypto.md. Contexts are thin wrappers over the OpenSSL-backed primitives in crypto.h.
