ara::phm — conformance with AUTOSAR AP R25-11 (SWS Platform Health Management)

Source: AUTOSAR_AP_SWS_PlatformHealthManagement.pdf (R25-11).

Method and limits. The requirement ids below are the ones that could be read from the R25-11 text (chapters 5 and 7: functional behaviour). Chapter 8 (the formal C++ API tables: exact signatures, PhmErrc numeric values, TypeOfSupervision values) was not retrievable in this audit, so API-shape rows are marked Unverified instead of being guessed. Ids not listed here were not read and are not claimed.

Fallback release. R22-11 (AUTOSAR_SWS_PlatformHealthManagement.pdf) was read as a fallback. Its Chapter 8 is also only headings in the extracted text, so signatures and numeric values stay Unverified; it additionally confirms SWS_PHM_00101 (the supervision parameter carries the TypeOfSupervision that caused kEXPIRED), SWS_PHM_01343 (elementary statuses kOK, kDEACTIVATED, kEXPIRED, kFAILED) and SWS_PHM_00216 (global statuses kOK, kDEACTIVATED, kFAILED, kEXPIRED, kSTOPPED) - all matching the implementation.

Status legend: Implemented, Partial, Missing, Deviation (known, intentional or forced), Unverified (could not be checked against the text).
