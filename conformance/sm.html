ara::sm - conformance to AUTOSAR AP R25-11 (SWS State Management)

Audited against AUTOSAR_AP_SWS_StateManagement.pdf (R25-11).

Verification limits. Only the first ~100 000 characters of the PDF could be read (chapters 1-7 and parts of 8). Method signatures, argument types and the numeric values of error codes (chapter 8/9) were not readable, so they are marked Unverified. Requirement ids below are copied from the text that was read; nothing was inferred.

Status legend: Implemented (behaviour present and unit-tested), Partial, Missing, Deviation (present but different from the spec / obsolete), Unverified (cannot be confirmed from the readable text).

What changed in this audit

  • ara/sm/update_request/update_session.h - the session rules, thread-safe, unit-tested (12 tests in Testing/sm).
  • updaterequest_skeleton.h previously could not link (an undefined static activeSession, undeclared bodies for VerifyUpdate/PrepareRollback, a reference to a non-existent StateManager::EM) and its ResetMachine could never succeed. It now delegates to UpdateSession and is compile/link-checked by a test.
  • kOperationRejected added to errorDomains.

Suggested next steps

  1. Instantiate the UpdateRequest skeleton in App/SM, install a reset handler, call Save/Load at start-up.
  2. Replace the hard-coded FSM by a configurable StateMachine model and RequestTransition.
  3. Read chapters 8/9 of the PDF (needs a full copy of the document) to confirm signatures and error values.
